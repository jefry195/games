Mahjong Solitaire

Match free tile pairs. Clear the board to win.

Mahjong Solitaire

Easy
Tiles: 0

How to play

Clear the board by matching pairs of identical tiles. A tile is free if nothing covers it and at least one side (left or right) is open.

  • Tap a free tile to select it, then tap its matching free partner to remove both.
  • Tap a different free tile to change selection.
  • Hint highlights a valid pair (limited by level).
  • Undo restores the last removed pair (limited by level).
  • Reshuffle redistributes remaining tiles on the same slots.
